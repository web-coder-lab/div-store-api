Privacy Policy

Div Store · Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Div Store processes information when you use the application, admin tools, and APIs. We focus on data needed to run the catalog, handle submissions, deliver downloads, and protect the platform.

Scope

This policy covers Div Store’s own processing. It does not cover third-party apps you install from the catalog. Those apps have separate policies.

Catalog data

We process application names, package identifiers, descriptions, versions, icons, screenshots, sizes, categories, feature flags, scan status, download counts, and rating summaries.

Submission data

Developers and companies may provide contact emails, organization names, websites, descriptive text, and package metadata when submitting software.

Binary packages

Application packages may be stored so approved titles can be delivered to users through download links.

Reviews

If you post a review, we process the name you share, your rating, your text, and the time it was submitted. Reviews are visible to other users.

Technical data

We may process IP addresses, coarse client indicators, timestamps, and request paths to enforce rate limits, investigate abuse, and keep the Service available.

Admin access data

Operators use administrative credentials. Those credentials authorize privileged catalog actions and are not used for advertising.

How we use data

We use information to operate the catalog, review submissions, compute aggregate statistics, deliver download locations, secure the Service, contact submitters when needed, and comply with law.

Storage model

Durable catalog records are stored as structured data in private project repositories that act as the system of record. Servers do not keep a separate long-term catalog database only on local disk.

Release artifacts

Package binaries are stored as versioned release artifacts. Listing records may include the download location for those artifacts.

History and deletion

Repository history may keep earlier versions of files. Removing a live listing may not erase every historical copy at once. Contact support for help with deletion requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with infrastructure providers that host repositories or run servers, when required by law, or to protect users and the Service.

Public listings

Information included in a public listing is visible to people who use the Service.

After you download

Once you install a third-party app, that developer’s privacy practices apply to any further collection by their software.

Admin browser storage

The admin interface may keep a session token in browser storage so operators stay signed in on that device.

Retention

Catalog and submission records are kept while needed to operate the Service and for security or dispute needs. Technical logs are usually shorter-lived unless investigation requires more.

Your requests

You may ask to remove a review you wrote or a listing you control, subject to verification and technical limits of repository storage.

Security measures

We use administrative authentication, HTTPS where available, rate limiting, and separation of public reads from privileged writes. No system is perfectly secure.

International processing

Infrastructure may run in more than one country. Using the Service may involve processing in those locations under applicable legal rules.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under thirteen, or a higher age required where you live. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

Regional rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete certain information, or to object to some processing. Contact support to make a request.

Verification

We may need to verify identity before fulfilling a privacy request. Some requests can be limited for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Ranking signals

Download counts and similar signals may be calculated automatically from activity. We do not use fully automated systems for legal or similarly significant decisions about people without human involvement where required.

Policy updates

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy where allowed by law.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support channel published in the application or on the official project page for Div Store.

Related terms

Rules for using the platform are also described in the Terms of Service. Please read both documents.

Additional notes on repository storage

Because catalog files live in a versioned repository, operators with repository access may see historical states of listings. Access to those repositories is restricted to project credentials.

If a package is replaced, older release assets may remain available until they are manually removed from the artifact host according to operational practice.

Communications

If you submit a contact email with a package submission, we may use it to ask clarifying questions about the listing or to notify you about approval outcomes.

We do not use submission emails for unrelated marketing lists.

Do not track

The core Service does not change behavior based on browser Do Not Track signals because primary interaction is through the application and API rather than third-party ad tracking on a marketing site.

Data minimization

We aim to avoid collecting sensitive categories of personal data that are not required to run an application catalog. Please do not include unnecessary personal data in review text or listing descriptions.

Breach notification

If we become aware of a security incident that affects personal information we process, we will take steps consistent with applicable law, which may include notice to affected users or authorities where required.

Independent publishers

Publishers who submit apps remain responsible for the accuracy of their listings and for the behavior of their software after installation.

Language

If these materials are translated, the English version may be used to resolve ambiguity unless local law requires otherwise.
