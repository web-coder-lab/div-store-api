Privacy Policy

Div Store · Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Div Store handles information when you use the application, administrative tools, and related interfaces. We aim to process only what is needed to run the catalog, handle developer submissions, and keep the platform reasonably secure.

1. Scope

This policy covers information processed through official Div Store clients and APIs. It does not cover third-party applications you install from the catalog; those products follow their own policies.

2. Information we process

Catalog data. Application names, package identifiers, descriptions, versions, icons, screenshots, sizes, download counters, ratings summaries, and similar metadata required to display and manage listings.

Developer and company submissions. Contact emails, developer or company names, optional websites, submission notes, and package metadata. Binary packages are stored so approved titles can be delivered to users.

Reviews. Names and text that users choose to publish, star ratings, and timestamps.

Technical logs. IP addresses, coarse client indicators, timestamps, and request paths may be processed to enforce rate limits, investigate abuse, and maintain availability. These logs support operations rather than advertising profiles.

Administrative access. Operators authenticate with administrative credentials used only to authorize privileged catalog actions.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the catalog and download flows
  • To review, approve, reject, or remove submissions
  • To compute aggregate statistics such as downloads and ratings
  • To secure the Service and respond to misuse
  • To contact you about submissions when you provide an address
  • To meet legal obligations when required

4. Where information is stored

Durable catalog and submission records are kept as structured data in private project repositories used as the system of record. Application packages are stored as versioned release artifacts. Application servers process requests and persist durable state in those repositories rather than keeping a separate long-term database on the server disk.

Repository history may retain earlier versions of files. Removing a listing from the live catalog may not erase every historical copy immediately. Contact support if you need help with a deletion request.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with infrastructure providers that host repositories or run the API, when required by law, or to protect users and the Service. Public catalog fields are visible to people using the Service.

6. Downloads and third-party apps

When you download an application, the package is retrieved from artifact storage linked to that listing. After installation, the third-party app may collect data under its own policy. Div Store is not responsible for those practices.

7. Local storage in admin tools

The administrative interface may keep a session token in browser storage so operators stay signed in on that device. Core store browsing is not built around third-party advertising cookies.

8. Retention

Catalog and submission records are retained while needed to operate the Service and for security or dispute purposes. Technical logs are kept for shorter operational periods unless investigation or law requires longer. You may request removal of a review you posted or a listing you control, subject to verification and technical limits.

9. Security

Privileged actions require administrative authentication. HTTPS is used where the host provides it. Rate limits and separation between public reads and privileged writes are applied. No system is perfectly secure; protect your devices and credentials.

10. International processing

Infrastructure may operate in more than one country. Accessing the Service may involve processing in regions where hosts are located. Where required, appropriate legal mechanisms are used for cross-border transfers.

11. Children

The Service is not directed to children under thirteen, or under the minimum age required where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

12. Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete certain information, or to object to some processing. Send requests through the official support channel with enough detail to locate your records. Verification may be required. Some rights are limited where retention is necessary for security, law, or operation of a public listing you published.

13. Ranking signals

Download counts and similar ranking inputs may be calculated automatically from Service activity. We do not use fully automated systems to make legal or similarly significant decisions about individuals without human involvement where such involvement is required.

14. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect product or legal changes. The date at the top will be revised when that happens. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

15. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent through the support contact published in the application or on the official project page for Div Store.

If you do not agree with this policy, discontinue use of the Service. Platform rules of use are described in the Terms of Service.
